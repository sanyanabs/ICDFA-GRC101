# ICDFA GRC101 Labs

This repository contains my practical lab assignments completed during the International Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics Academy (ICDFA) Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC101) course.

## Topics Covered

- Governance Fundamentals
- Risk Management
- Risk Assessment
- Risk Mitigation
- Compliance
- Security Controls
- Risk Treatment Planning

These labs demonstrate my understanding of governance, risk management, and cybersecurity best practices while transitioning into a Security Engineering career.
